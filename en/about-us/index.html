About Us

From academic knowledge to professional field experience.

The Cooperative Training Hub platform (coophub.com.sa) is a Saudi platform specialized in the travel, tourism and religious tourism industry (Hajj and Umrah). It connects university students with accredited cooperative training (Co-op) opportunities inside and outside Saudi Arabia, and serves university students internationally.

Our Vision

To be the leading national platform and reference center for organizing and modernizing cooperative training tracks in travel, tourism, Hajj, Umrah and related technologies, and the main driver turning academic knowledge into professional field experience that leads the sectors of the future, in line with Saudi Vision 2030 and the 2027 labor-market targets.

Our Mission

To enable local and international university students to undertake academically accredited field-training experiences at leading organizations and companies in travel, tourism and services for the Guests of Allah, and to equip them with applied skills in the most in-demand fields such as travel technology and AI, Umrah and Hajj experience management, financial technology (FinTech), luxury hospitality and digital media — ensuring they meet graduation requirements and land their first job with confidence.
